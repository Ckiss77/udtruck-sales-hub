No double counting: Economic TCO includes depreciation (purchase price minus resale value) plus finance interest and operating costs. Loan principal is part of your payments, but is not added to depreciation. The full-term net cash flow reconciles to the same total if the operator receives the resale proceeds.

Net cash flow: first period includes down payment, each period includes finance payments and operating costs, and the final period deducts resale proceeds. Economic TCO instead uses straight-line depreciation and interest. Both sum to the same full-term total.

Model assumptions: Illustrative inputs only. Same annual mileage and loan term across trucks; prices, mileage and running costs held constant; effective-rate amortizing financing; straight-line depreciation; vehicle resale proceeds belong to operator. Electricity is calculated from kWh/km and optional charging losses. EVs require no diesel or AdBlue. Charging infrastructure, battery replacement, subsidies, VAT/taxes, inflation and carbon prices are excluded unless manually included in Other expenses. This is financed purchase / lease-to-own, not a true operating lease.
